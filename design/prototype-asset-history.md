# Keyboard prototype assets

The current local prototype retains the supplied Raycast geometry and UVs, with a new OpenKlack ivory/graphite/cobalt material palette, legend atlas, and base/RGB lighting bakes.
The procedural geometry replacement was rejected in visual review. The subsequent dark-texture adaptation was also rejected.
The [asset workflow](keyboard/README.md) retains untouched references and an editable Blender scene. Runtime recoloring and legend patches have been removed.
See [asset provenance](../packages/ui/assets/keyboard/PROVENANCE.md) for sources and redistribution status.
