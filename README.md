# OpenReaction

**Type `:tada:` in any text field on your Mac. Get 🎉.**

Free & open source · Mac native · No account · No telemetry

</div>

## Status

Early development. Not ready for daily use yet.

## What it does

- Slack/Discord-style `:shortcode:` emoji autocomplete in any app
- Inline picker at your caret — arrow keys to choose, Return or Tab to insert
- Stays out of password fields and apps that already expand shortcodes
- GIFs and stickers are planned

## Requirements

macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Uses Liquid Glass materials on macOS 26 Tahoe.

OpenReaction needs **Accessibility** and **Input Monitoring** permission to notice shortcodes as you type and insert the result into the app you are using. What you type is matched on your Mac and never stored or sent anywhere.
